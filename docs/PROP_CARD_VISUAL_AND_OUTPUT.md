## Exactly labeled card fields
- Header: event/league/date/time in America/Chicago, player/team/opponent, event identity and status, role/injury as-of time, publication cutoff, model/decision version.
- Opponent exploitation: team weakness, position weakness, individual assignment, opponent strength attacked, opportunity and distribution-shift hypothesis, signature and strongest opposing scenario.
- Independent prediction: sport-specific P50 and predictive range, recent L5/L10/L20 (and season/role baseline), source windows/sample sizes, **central raw-unit cushion and adverse cushion**, normalized cushion when valid, calibrated probability / modeled-only survival / lower bound with clear labels.
- MARKET: exact operator, threshold with `N+` vs `>` vs `<` semantics, timestamp and current quote only if verified; otherwise `TARGET PRICE ONLY — LIVE QUOTE PENDING`. Do not show a fabricated book/line or exact EV.
- Simulations: MC-1 BASELINE, MC-2 ADVERSARIAL, MC-3 SCENARIO/CORRELATION, each actual trials/hits/input and version; display `NOT RUN` when missing. Separate joint simulations for qualifying parlays.
- Decision: one PRIMARY/event, <=1 SAFER ALT, <=1 REPLACEMENT; distinct **FORECAST GRADE** and **WAGER STATUS**. Include evidence coverage, contradiction, risk/kill switch, fair/target price, and reason to HIT/MISS.
- Audit: original projection cushion is **not** actual postgame clearance. Preserve published threshold and all decision revisions; HIT/MISS/PUSH/VOID/PENDING, result source and settlement.

Rank research boards **highest to lowest supported estimated hit probability** (then adverse survival/evidence quality), not by start time unless explicitly requested. Do not mix uncalibrated model rates with calibrated hit probabilities or claim 100% cushion means certainty.

## Display validity
Data contracts, not image-generation aesthetics, govern correctness. Validate the player/team/league and event date before rendering. Keep labels within frames, avoid overlapping text, never place percentages in wrong rows, and attach an unmistakable HOLD when the matching current source/price is absent. A great-looking card must not mask a research-only candidate.
